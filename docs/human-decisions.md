# Okojo — Human Decision Record

**Author:** Jennifer Hicks, CAMS-RM, CGSS, PMP
**Purpose:** A consolidated record of the human decisions that directed this project — scope, architecture, domain design, data integrity, publication, and process. It complements `docs/DECISIONS.md` (which records *why* individual technical choices stand) by documenting *who decided*: the locus of creative and directive control throughout an AI-assisted build.
**Method note:** Okojo was built with AI assistance under a strict authority model: all AI output — code, plans, and recommendations alike — was advisory until reviewed and approved by the author. Build sessions operated under binding amendments and a hard rule that nothing reached the public repository without an explicit human clearance following a full pre-publish gate ritual. Decisions below are marked **[Originated]** (conceived and directed by the author) or **[Ratified]** (recommended in an advisory capacity, then reviewed, in several cases modified, and approved by the author). Both classes reflect human control; a number of advisory recommendations were overridden outright, and those overrides are noted.
**Last updated:** 2026-08-04

## 1. Inception and scope

- **D-001 [Originated] — Project premise.** An agentic AI co-pilot for financial-crime investigations at a crypto exchange, built from scratch on fully synthetic data, in public, solo, at ~20 hours/week. The problem set derives entirely from the author's professional casework in sanctions investigations, financial-crime compliance, and counterterrorism.
- **D-002 [Originated] — The ten pain points.** The candidate problem list evaluated at inception is the author's own documented inventory of investigation pain points from practice. Every capability in the system traces back to one of them.
- **D-066 [Originated] — Independent research into agentic decision points.** Before and during the build, the author independently researched viable decision points for AI agents (LangGraph-style orchestration) across the investigation production cycle, and sketched rough architecture-map drafts by hand, on paper. The system's bounded-decision architecture — where in the pipeline an agent decides, and what it is permitted to decide — reflects that offline work. *(ID assigned at record time; placed here by topic.)*
- **D-003 [Ratified] — One flagship, not a suite.** Approved the recommendation to build a single coherent system with an explicit MVP boundary and documented roadmap rather than several small demos.
- **D-004 [Originated] — Exclusion of the privileged-account pain point from public scope.** Ruled that "AI that routes around VIP protections" is unpublishable out of context; the underlying insight was instead inverted into a public control (see D-023).
- **D-005 [Ratified] — Committed v1.0 capstone.** Approved promoting the designation-triggered remediation sweep from roadmap to committed scope on a payoff-to-marginal-cost argument.
- **D-006 [Originated] — Naming and identity.** Selected the project name and logo; approved all public naming throughout.
- **D-007 [Ratified] — Tech stack.** Approved LangGraph, Chroma/FAISS, DuckDB/SQLite, networkx + pyvis, RapidFuzz, pydantic, Streamlit, pytest, and the pinned-dependency policy.
- **D-008 [Originated] — Licensing.** MIT license and sole authorship through v1.0. At the v1.0 release tag, ruled to keep MIT and continue accepting no external code contributions (sole copyright preserved, the relicensing option retained). At v1.1.0, changed the Licensed Work (v1.1.0 and later) to the Business Source License 1.1, with v1.0.0 and earlier remaining MIT; the author set the parameters — non-production use free, production or commercial use requires a license, Change Date 2030-08-03, Change License MIT.

## 2. Standing rules (author-imposed, binding on every session)

- **D-009 [Originated] — Synthetic and public data only.** No real person, address, document, or customer record enters the repository, ever. All scenario data is generated; all reference data is public (Elliptic, IBM IT-AML, OFAC, FinCEN advisories).
- **D-010 [Originated] — Publication gates.** A layered pre-publish ritual — line-level content sweeps, a four-surface history sweep (working tree, full history, commit messages, archives), secrets/PII checks, and protected-term classes maintained only in local untracked configuration — runs before every push. The author personally dictated the protected-term classes, their exclusions, and the rule that certain false-positive-prone terms are investigated on every hit rather than allowlisted.
- **D-011 [Originated] — Human-in-the-loop, always.** The agent prepares; a person decides and files. No send paths, no execution paths, proposal-only outputs across every capability.
- **D-012 [Originated] — The audit trail is the centerpiece.** Tamper-evident, append-only logging of every access, action, and decision, with provenance — elevated from feature to thesis by the author on the basis of professionally observed governance-capture failure modes.
- **D-013 [Originated] — Grounding contract.** Every asserted fact must trace to a retrieved record; uncitable statements are rejected, fail-closed.
- **D-014 [Originated] — Calibrated language.** Public text and system output use *proposes / surfaces / drafts / flags*; overclaiming terms are banned and, per D-062, mechanically enforced.
- **D-015 [Originated] — Naming hygiene.** The device identifier is `device_fingerprint`; internal-industry schema terms are barred from the repository.
- **D-016 [Originated] — Push authority.** Nothing is pushed without the full gate ritual passing and the author's explicit, per-push clearance. Verified commit counts are mandatory in every report following an author-caught discrepancy.
- **D-017 [Originated] — Session pacing.** At most two slices land per build session; only the author may extend the cap, and did so explicitly on the occasions it happened. Every session ends with a handoff document.
- **D-018 [Originated] — Full-access rule.** After a review incident in which analysis proceeded on partial access to referenced material, the author imposed a standing rule: any inability to read referenced material in full must be surfaced immediately; no work proceeds on partial understanding. A companion rule requires analysis reasoning to be shown *before* design records are updated.
- **D-019 [Originated] — Source-of-truth rule for public documents.** Edits to public documents must be drafted against the live published versions, never local or private copies. Imposed after a divergence was caught between a private draft and the published, already-scrubbed public document.
- **D-020 [Originated] — Advisory text is gated like generated text.** All text supplied by advisory roles is subject to the same pre-publish sweeps before approval for any tracked file — a rule added when the gates caught an advisory-supplied draft that did not meet the publication standard. The layered process is deliberately designed so that no single participant, human or AI, is trusted unilaterally.

## 3. Scenario and data-integrity decisions

- **D-021 [Originated] — Scenario register.** The synthetic scenario remains in a single oil/sanctions-evasion register; no terror-financing-styled content is added to the public demo. The synthetic designation program label derives from openly cited public FinCEN advisories — an explicit author ruling.
- **D-022 [Originated] — Determinism as policy.** The generator is seeded and regenerates byte-identically; only the generator is committed, never its output. The evaluation answer key is kept in lockstep with the generator by rule.
- **D-023 [Originated] — The privileged-tag control.** A planted "privileged / internal account" tag is something the system flags for review and never obeys — the author's inversion of an observed governance-capture failure into an enforced control.
- **D-024 [Originated] — Typological plausibility.** Planted evaluation cases must reflect real-world designation typologies. Applied concretely when the author rejected an employee-designation plant as implausible ("an extremely rare and unique occurrence") and redirected the design to a name-only relative listing.
- **D-025 [Ratified] — Contamination controls.** Approved and enforced the standing checks that new personas reuse existing jurisdictions (advisory-ranking isolation) and that new names are verified token-distinctive against the transaction-remark corpus before regeneration — both rules born from author-reviewed incidents.
- **D-026 [Originated] — Fictional geography.** All territory, country, carrier, and telephone identifiers in the geo capability are invented and provably inert (ISO user-assigned code ranges; an unassigned ITU prefix), following the author's requirement that the public repository never renders judgments about real places, companies, or people. The author personally eyeballed and approved every invented label, and ordered a carrier renamed when an external check showed the proposed name resembled real telecom businesses.
- **D-027 [Originated] — Name vetting as routine.** Every invented persona, entity, and public symbol name in the later phases passed an author eyeball plus external collision checks before use; the author repeatedly chose rename-to-zero over log-as-benign when public-index collisions appeared (see D-063).
- **D-080 [Originated/Ratified] — Gas-funding must render at the address level.** A live-demo review caught the network graph drawing gas-funding edges *from the subject's exchange-account node* (the Allison Hill star) *to* funded wallet addresses. **[Originated]** the author flagged this as impossible and supplied the domain constraint, verified against primary protocol sources: gas is paid at the **address** level in **native coin** (on Tron, a TRC-20 USDT transfer burns Energy that an unstaked address must hold TRX to pay), and **no** sponsored-fee mechanism (Tron delegation, ERC-4337 paymasters, Solana feePayer) lets an off-chain custodial *account* pay gas for a self-hosted wallet — so a gas funder is always an on-chain address, never a uid, in the data **and** in every rendered surface, and gas rows are native-coin (kept qualitative, never pegged to governance-mutable fee constants). **[Originated]** the standing **vendor-sourcing rule**: on-chain links and controller attribution are attributed to the institution's blockchain-intelligence feed (synthetic), and clustering heuristics are cited to vendor-documented practice (address-clustering / funding-pattern glossaries) rather than asserted bare. **[Ratified]** the **three-step render rule**: the graph renders the inference as `account →(controls, cited attribution)→ funder wallet →(TRX top-up, observed chain fact)→ funded wallets`, with the account node never sourcing an on-chain edge and the attribution edge visually distinct from the gas observation. Investigation established the data was already address→address (the impossible edge was a render-time attribution *collapse*), so the fix was render-only under STOP-on-drift — every network metric, exposure set, and scorecard byte-identical. *(ID assigned at record time; placed here by topic.)*
- **D-079 [Originated/Ratified] — Two-record transaction data model.** A live-demo review caught an on-chain transfer rendered with a free-text remark, which a TRC-20 transfer (no memo) cannot carry. **[Originated]** the author ruled to fix the model *properly* — even across sessions — over any band-aid, after a proposed one-row relabel was proven to sever the shell→sanctioned exposure path (the set collapsed 8→3) and halted under STOP-on-drift. The author supplied the domain constraint from operational experience: withdrawals settle on-chain **from an omnibus exchange hot wallet, never a customer-attributed address**; deposits arrive at a per-customer deposit address; the exposure walk traverses internal and chain records as one graph, counting each movement once. The author further ruled that the two betraying free-text tells relocate to **customer address-book labels** (a saved/whitelisted-address note), the realistic off-chain home for such text — not to fabricated exchange rows. **[Ratified]** the Option-4 design (one transaction table with a `record_kind` discriminator and settlement links; a single omnibus hot wallet; on-chain settlement legs excluded from the flow/value ledger so every exposure set and dollar figure is preserved by construction; the Tell Miner reads remarks and address-book labels alike) with its nine reviewed sub-rulings, under standing STOP-on-drift and the enumerated-gold-change discipline. *(ID assigned at record time; placed here by topic.)*

## 4. Domain designs contributed from professional practice (Phase 8)

The five-part sanctions expansion is the project's deepest human contribution: its designs encode the author's professional investigative methods, several of which exist in no public tooling.

- **D-028 [Originated] — The expansion itself.** On reviewing the walking system, the author paused the plan and dictated a substantial expansion grounded in her sanctions-screening experience: post-onboarding designation screening is a manual gap; vendor screening covers onboarding only; the remainder was investigator labor. The five-part structure (flow sweep; designation-model extension and cross-list early warning; identity resolution; geo triangulation; counterparty lifecycle) was built to her testimony.
- **D-029 [Originated] — Cross-list early warning.** From the author's counterterrorism-derived practice of consulting smaller national designation lists: national lists publish names years before major-list designations, and smaller states frequently track individuals at higher granularity. The list-source registry (with explicit ingested/not-ingested flags for visible absence), the lead-time window measurement, and the name-only granularity plant all encode this method. The author supplied the operating principle verbatim: *a risk signal with a timestamp, never a legal claim.*
- **D-030 [Originated] — Family and associate proximity.** The author identified relatives and close associates of designated parties as first-order risk indicators and directed the proximity-ring design, including the rules that kinship is never asserted as fact, that dormancy is not innocence (a dormant relative surfaces; an active stranger does not), and that activity volume is never a weighting signal.
- **D-031 [Ratified] — Corroboration discipline.** Approved the three-outcome corroboration decision (confirmed / needs-human / dismissed-collision) with the author-endorsed rule that an absent field is unknown, never a mismatch, and that dismissal reasons are recorded.
- **D-032 [Originated] — Multi-script name variants.** The variant-screening capability responds to the author's described casework reality: customers self-romanize their names, and official lists publish variants that match none of them. The author selected the showcase scripts (Cyrillic + Arabic).
- **D-033 [Originated] — Region-exclusive carrier signal.** From the author's geofencing casework: a mobile carrier operating only inside a sanctioned region is itself a location indicator. The `exclusive_carriers` registry, the carrier field, and a carrier-only planted case exist because the author contributed this technique.
- **D-034 [Originated] — The one-signal rule.** Any single location signal surfaces an account for review — the author's testimony, applied verbatim as the surfacing rule.
- **D-035 [Originated] — VPN discipline and the slip.** VPN use is never location evidence, only a contradiction marker; but a bare regional IP captured during a gap in otherwise-continuous VPN use is a higher-value observation than a routine hit. Both rules are the author's, from practice.
- **D-036 [Originated] — The ambiguous traveler.** The author posed the scenario — a national of a sanctioned-adjacent country onboarded abroad, holding a now-expired residency document, with a single VPN-slip IP — and it became the geo capability's centerpiece evaluation case. Its correct outcome, an EDD request for information, encodes the author's judgment that when the totality of evidence genuinely cannot resolve, the honest proposal is the action that resolves it: ask.
- **D-037 [Originated] — Document staleness as a modifier.** The author identified expired identity documents as an evidentiary indicator from casework and directed the design that records expiry as a *staleness modifier* — degrading counter-evidence, never itself indicating presence — with a dual flag for the upstream KYC-refresh control gap.
- **D-038 [Originated] — The proposal ladder.** Directed the net-presence scoring rule mapping signal totality to the author's real-world action menu (no action / EDD RFI / withdrawal-only restriction / trade-and-withdrawal block / full block and escalate), with author-imposed riders: the no-action outcome must remain visible to reviewers, and the centerpiece case must fall out of the rule arithmetically rather than being special-cased.
- **D-039 [Originated] — The action menu itself.** The four graduated restriction actions are the author's actual practitioner menu, contributed from experience.
- **D-040 [Originated] — Counterparty lifecycle and the no-auto-unblock rule.** The lifecycle design (notify → acknowledge → verify stop → propose) follows the author's practitioner experience with counterparty-designation events. The hard rule that no automated process ever reverses a human hold — an unblock exists only as a proposal gated on verified acknowledgment — encodes the author's direct professional experience that automated unblocking must never outrun per-case human review. The author also confirmed the stop-dealing cut is measured from acknowledgment, not designation.
- **D-041 [Originated] — Notification disclosure scope.** What a customer notification may and may not say (a public counterparty designation and a terms obligation: sayable; methods, investigation existence, law-enforcement interest: never) reflects the author's tipping-off discipline, enforced mechanically on rendered text.
- **D-042 [Ratified] — Identity-review RFI contact rules.** Approved the rule that only the unresolvable-candidate class is ever contacted; a corroborated hit and a cleared collision never are.
- **D-073 [Originated/Ratified] — v1.1 case-side designation check.** [Originated] the requirement that whatever is prudent for an investigator to know about their subject be apparent in case mode; the nothing-excluded override that brought territory designations into scope; and the four-point subject-first Sanctions-tab specification (subject header, posture badge, network notice, and a subject-scoped layout above the ledger-wide context). [Ratified] the three-state calibrated badge (never the bare word "sanctioned"; a binary was rejected), the dismissed-collision rendering (green with an always-visible, cited dismissal line), the unconditional proof-of-screening audit record with corroboration embedded in its payload, and the both-ways proof strategy (live persona states plus a clean-state assertion). *(ID assigned at record time; placed here by topic.)*
- **D-078 [Originated] — Screening coverage-gap check.** Directed the institution-level coverage-gap check: measure the customer base's whole geographic footprint against the enabled list-source regimes and surface the mismatch as a standing, cited signal — the automated form of a coverage argument from the author's practice. Ruled that nationality join residence and KYC-issuing country as a third, separately-counted footprint leg, being necessary to an investigation and the leg that surfaces a jurisdiction with no enabled coverage that residence alone would miss. Overrode the advisory recommendation to compute the assessment at render time only, directing instead that it write its own separate hash-chained audit-trail family. Directed that its coverage policy live in a new versioned configuration with its own methodology document and doc-to-code anti-drift guard, reading the frozen sweep registry rather than duplicating it. *(ID assigned at record time; placed here by topic.)*

## 6. Verification and quality decisions

- **D-052 [Originated] — Evaluation-first policy.** Every capability ships with an evaluation against the generated answer key; approved planted-trap designs (decoys, anti-replay exclusions, precision foils) throughout.
- **D-053 [Originated] — The falsification amendment.** Prompted by the author's concern about AI verification theater, a binding amendment now requires every new capability evaluation to include a *demonstrated* falsification — an assertion shown to fail when the capability is disabled or its input perturbed, with the failing run's output quoted in the report. Spot-audits (deliberately breaking a threshold to watch the evaluation go red) were added at the author's direction.
- **D-054 [Ratified] — Structural vs. capability harnesses.** Ratified the ruling distinguishing byte-frozen capability scorecards (any movement halts work) from structural coverage harnesses that grow with the scenario as announced deltas.
- **D-055 [Ratified] — Wall-clock discipline.** Ratified the single, permanent adjustment of the hard suite-runtime line (150s → 200s) with the rationale recorded, and the standing requirement of clean, nothing-concurrent measurement before any runtime judgment.
- **D-056 [Originated] — CI as independent verification.** Approved continuous integration as the first slice of the narrator phase so all subsequent work built under independent green. When the first CI run exposed a latent cross-platform nondeterminism, the author cleared the diagnose-first ruling: root-cause and fix the portability defect properly, with platform-masking options (xfail, platform-pinned CI) rejected. The red-to-green run pair is preserved in the record as the honest story.
- **D-057 [Originated] — Guard activation.** Ruled that a calibration guard existing since Phase 1 without a live call site be wired fail-closed into the drafting pipeline — a control with the shape but not the substance of enforcement being exactly what the project's standards forbid — after first proving activation moved nothing.
- **D-069 [Ratified] — Phase-10 dependency ordering and security posture.** Ratified that the security pass gates the deploy and the provenance/snippet-level SCA gates the release tag, together with the security-tooling posture: pinned development requirements, a scheduled non-badge audit workflow, inline justified suppressions approved per instance, and a development-environment refresh proven inert before reliance. *(ID assigned at record time; placed here by topic.)*
- **D-075 [Originated] — Verification-honesty standing rule.** A required verification is DONE or NOT DONE; a check that cannot be completed is reported NOT DONE with the blocker and exact manual steps for the author; adjacent evidence (local runs, tests, same-code reasoning) is labeled as such and never substitutes for the required check; only the author may accept a substitute. Adopted after the author personally caught a verification being self-downgraded, and had the record corrected. *(ID assigned at record time; placed here by topic.)*
- **D-076 [Originated/Ratified] — Wall-clock thermal disposition and CI portability.** [Originated] disposed of a hard-line wall-clock crossing as warm-machine thermal state, with the independent cold-CI datapoint established as the standing arbiter (complements D-055). [Ratified] the CI-red portability ruling (see D-056): root-cause the cross-platform defect; platform-masking options rejected; the red-to-green run pair preserved as the honest record. *(ID assigned at record time; placed here by topic.)*

## 7. Publication, register, and record decisions

- **D-058 [Originated] — Phase-boundary status integrity.** Status surfaces move together and truthfully: an interim "in progress" update was authorized when the repository gained public visitors (accuracy for visitors outweighing boundary aesthetics), while the "complete" designation remained reserved for full sign-off. Conservative underclaims were preferred wherever timing was ambiguous.

Personally caught residual instances the first automated sweep missed, ordered the sweep's term set widened, and ruled on every borderline hit individually (including deliberate keeps).

- **D-061 [Originated] — Sequencing for the launch narrative.** Reordered the roadmap so the Audit Narrator (making the tamper-evident record *reviewable*, scoped by the author to all chain families) ships before any recorded walkthrough — the demo should show the finished system, narrator included.

- **D-063 [Originated] — Rename-to-zero standard.** Where public-index checks found even benign name collisions for new public symbols, the author repeatedly chose renaming to zero external hits over logging the collision, with pre-push history rebuilds so abandoned names never entered a published blob.
- **D-064 [Originated] — Roadmap curation.** Personally approved each post-v1.0 roadmap item, including the coverage-gap check (the automated form of a screening-coverage argument from the author's practice) and a production-posture line on the API-shaped design, added at the author's direction.

- **D-067 [Originated] — Trademark marking convention.** Adopted a prominent-use marking convention for the Okojo name and logo: the trademark mark appears on the first and most prominent use per public surface, not on every mention; the registered mark is never used before registration.
- **D-068 [Originated] — Documentation-governance principle.** Public documents state capabilities; private working notes hold intentions.
- **D-071 [Ratified] — Out-of-band-commit norm.** Ratified the standing norm that any commit landing outside a build session receives a post-hoc gate sweep at the next session open.
- **D-072 [Originated] — Repository-topic curation.** Every listed repository topic must be a claim the repository can back; detection and monitoring tags were declined on that basis.
- **D-077 [Originated] — Status and record currency.** Directed the README Sources section, authorized a full stale-status documentation sweep at the v1.1 boundary, and promoted the coverage-gap check from the roadmap to built.

## 8. Authority model (summary)

Every build session operated under a written kickoff approved by the author; every slice plan was adjudicated before build; every judgment call made in-flight was disclosed for after-the-fact review, and several were reversed; every push required the author's explicit clearance after a documented gate ritual; and every capability's design traces either to the author's professional practice or to a recommendation the author reviewed and approved. The system's distinguishing designs — cross-list early warning, granularity-aware screening, proximity risk, the one-signal rule, the carrier signal, the VPN-slip citation, staleness handling, the graduated action ladder, the no-auto-unblock rule, and the audit-trail-as-centerpiece thesis — originate in the author's investigative practice and exist in the codebase because the author put them there.
